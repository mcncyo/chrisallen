## Desktop Environment

### Pop!_OS Experience

After over a decade of using Windows, I made the switch to **Pop!_OS** on my primary workstation. Here's my experience so far:

- **Why Pop!_OS?**
  - Based on Ubuntu LTS for stability
  - Excellent NVIDIA support out of the box
  - Tiling window manager (thanks to Pop Shell)
  - Clean, polished desktop environment

## Web Hosting Solution

### Migration to DigitalOcean

After years of self-hosting my websites, I've migrated to **DigitalOcean Droplets** for improved reliability. Here's why I made this decision:

- **Uptime**: Target of 99.9% uptime (vs ~95% with home hosting)
- **Performance**: Global CDN and SSD storage
- **Security**: Automatic OS updates and firewalls
- **Backups**: Automated daily snapshots

### Current Stack

- **Operating System**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS
- **Web Server**: Nginx with HTTP/3 (QUIC) support
- **SSL**: Let's Encrypt with auto-renewal
- **Deployment**: Automated CI/CD pipeline
- **Monitoring**: Uptime monitoring with status page

### Cost Analysis

| Service | Monthly Cost | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| DigitalOcean Droplet | $6 | Basic droplet with 1GB RAM |
| Backup Snapshots | $1 | Weekly backups retained for 4 weeks |
| Domain Names | $20/year | Multiple domains with privacy protection |
| **Total** | **~$10/month** | Reliable hosting with room to scale |
