- **Budget-friendly** (Under $50)

The KINGJOY 75" seemed to check all these boxes, so I decided to take the plunge.

## Performance Review

### Build Quality
For a budget tripod, the build quality is impressive. The aluminum alloy legs feel durable, and the plastic components don't feel cheap. The flip locks are sturdy and hold the legs firmly in place.

### Stability
At full extension (75"), there's some wobble, but that's expected with most tripods in this price range. For best results, I recommend extending the center column only when necessary.

### Ease of Use
The quick-release plate makes it simple to attach and detach your camera. The phone mount is easy to adjust and holds devices securely.

### Portability
Weighing just 3.3 lbs, this tripod is easy to carry around. The included carrying bag has a shoulder strap, making it convenient for travel.

**Rating: 4.2/5**
